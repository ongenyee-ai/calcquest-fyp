# Learning Objectives

The prototype will focus on the calculus topic of differentiation. The learning objectives are designed to support undergraduate engineering students in practising foundational differentiation concepts through short, game-style exercises.

## Learning Objective 1: Conceptual Understanding
Explain the derivative as the rate of change of a function and interpret its meaning in simple engineering-related contexts.

## Learning Objective 2: Power Rule
Apply the power rule to differentiate polynomial functions.

## Learning Objective 3: Product and Quotient Rules
Differentiate functions involving products and quotients using the appropriate rules.

## Learning Objective 4: Chain Rule
Apply the chain rule to differentiate composite functions.

## Learning Objective 5: Mixed Rule Selection
Select and apply the correct differentiation rule when solving mixed differentiation questions.

## Learning Objective 6: Error Correction
Use hints and feedback to identify common mistakes in differentiation, such as rule misuse, algebraic errors, or incorrect simplification.
